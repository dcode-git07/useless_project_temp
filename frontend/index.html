👅 The Chai Interrogation

You only need to taste the chai. No recipe knowledge required.

1. How was the sweetness?

Too sweet, too little, or just right?

2. How was the milk balance?

Too milky, too little milk, or perfectly balanced?

3. How strong was the tea?

Weak, medium, strong, or full-on kadak?

4. What did the flavour taste like?

Plain, nicely spiced, or overloaded with spices?

5. One final question...

How good was that sip? Would you happily drink another cup?
